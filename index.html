About Oasis Unit

Empowerment Through Empathy

Founded at Government Engineering College (GEC) Idukki, Oasis is a student-driven initiative operating under the guiding principles of community-based palliative care in Kerala. We instill social responsibility and compassionate care among engineering undergraduates.

Youth Awareness

Conducting seminars, orientation workshops, and interactive sessions for college students to build an empathetic youth mindset.

Companion Care

Visiting bedridden and chronic patients in Painavu to provide warm companionship, friendly conversations, and emotional support.

Material Pooling

Organizing campus donation drives for essential medical items, water beds, adult diapers, and daily care kits for needy families.

Home Care Support

Collaborating with local government palliative nurses and community doctors during regular home-care patient rounds.

Outreach & Programs

Our Key Initiatives

Discover how Oasis GEC Idukki brings tangible relief, festival joy, and awareness across the district.

Flagship Fest

Harsham Palliative Fest

The annual palliative care awareness festival hosted at GEC Idukki featuring cultural events, seminars, street plays, and fundraising drives for care equipment.

Community Visits

Old Age Home Outreach

Bi-weekly visits to elder care homes in Idukki. Student volunteers share companion care, record life stories, and supply essential care kits.

Festive Joy

Festive Care Drives

Spreading Onam and Christmas cheer directly to homebound bedridden patients with customized feast kits, traditional wear, and musical visits.

Annual Palliative Fest

HARSHAM FEST 2026

Harsham is more than an annual festival; it is a movement of youthful empathy. Every year, GEC Idukki comes alive with student competitions, art installations, medical relief rallies, and workshops dedicated to honoring palliative care heroes.
