Welcome to OASIS Pain and Palliative Care Unit at Government Engineering College, Idukki — where the precision of young engineering minds meets the soft touch of raw human empathy. Healing Hands, Resilient Hearts We transform ambition into altruism, empowering students to deliver tender companion care, vital relief drives, and restored dignity to palliative patients across Idukki and Kerala.

About Oasis Unit

True education does not merely train the mind, It awakens the soul to the pain of others.

Established in 2010 by Dr. Latha Raj, OASIS stands as the beating heart of social conscience at GEC Idukki. By transforming engineering students into messengers of palliative relief, this unit has transcended institutional boundaries to touch countless lives in the wider community. OASIS is a living reminder that care is an action, hope is a shared responsibility, and young hands hold the power to heal broken spirits.

Our Vision

Driven by purpose, this open club instills the sacred value of palliative care in young hearts, rapidly scaling unexpected heights to turn student empathy into an unstoppable movement of hope.

Companion Care

Visiting bedridden and chronic patients in Idukki to provide warm companionship, friendly conversations, and emotional support.

Material Pooling

Organizing campus donation drives for essential medical items, water beds, adult diapers, and daily care kits for needy families.

Home Care Support

Collaborating with local government palliative nurses and community doctors during regular home-care patient rounds.

Outreach & Programs

Our Key Initiatives

Discover how Oasis GEC Idukki brings tangible relief, festival joy, and awareness across the district.

Flagship Fest

Harsham Palliative Fest

The annual palliative care awareness festival hosted at GEC Idukki featuring cultural events, seminars, street plays, and fundraising drives for care equipment.

Community Visits

Old Age Home Outreach

Bi-weekly visits to elder care homes in Idukki. Student volunteers share companion care, record life stories, and supply essential care kits.

Festive Joy

Festive Care Drives

Spreading Onam and Christmas cheer directly to homebound bedridden patients with customized feast kits, traditional wear, and musical visits.
