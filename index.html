About Oasis Unit

Awakening Empathy Through Action

Companion Care

Visiting bedridden and chronic patients across Idukki to provide warm companionship, friendly conversations, and dedicated emotional support.

Material Pooling

Organizing campus donation drives for essential medical items, water beds, adult diapers, and daily comfort kits for vulnerable families.

Home Care Support

Collaborating alongside local government palliative nurses and community doctors during regular home-care medical rounds.

“True education does not merely train the mind; it awakens the soul to the pain of others.”

— Oasis Unit Guiding Statement

Established in 2010 by Dr. Latha Raj, OASIS stands as the social conscience of GEC Idukki. By involving engineering students in palliative relief, the unit transcends institutional boundaries to support patients and family caregivers throughout the district.

Our Vision

To cultivate empathy and civic responsibility in young engineers, inspiring lifelong service and expanding compassionate care throughout the community.

Outreach & Programs

Our Key Initiatives

Bringing tangible relief, festival joy, and awareness to bedridden patients and elder homes across Idukki.

Flagship Fest

Harsham Palliative Fest

The annual palliative awareness festival at GEC Idukki featuring interactive workshops, street plays, and care kit fundraising.

Community Visits

Old Age Home Outreach

Fortnightly visits to elder care homes in Idukki. Student volunteers provide companion care, listen to life stories, and supply comfort kits.

Festive Joy

Festive Care Drives

Spreading festive warmth to homebound bedridden patients with customized Onam feast kits, traditional wear, and musical visits.
